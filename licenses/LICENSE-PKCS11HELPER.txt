pkcs11-helper (libpkcs11-helper-1.dll)
======================================

libpkcs11-helper-1.dll is the PKCS#11 helper library bundled inside the
OpenVPN for Windows binary distribution (used by openvpn.exe for smartcard /
PKCS#11 token support).
